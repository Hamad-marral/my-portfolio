WhatsApp Business Automation

An AI-powered WhatsApp assistant that answers customers around the clock, qualifies leads and alerts the team when a person is needed.

The Challenge

The business could not answer every WhatsApp message quickly, so many potential customers went elsewhere.

The Solution

A WhatsApp Business API bot, powered by an OpenAI agent in n8n, replies to customers, saves new leads to the CRM and notifies the team on urgent cases.

The Result

The assistant is available 24/7, responses are about 80% faster, and the number of leads tripled.
