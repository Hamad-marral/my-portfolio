AI Lead Automation System

An automated lead-processing workflow that captures leads, qualifies them with AI, pushes them to the CRM and sends personalized follow-ups.

The Challenge

Leads came in from forms and chats but were copied into the CRM by hand, so follow-ups were slow and some leads were lost.

The Solution

An n8n workflow captures each lead, an OpenAI model scores and classifies it, the lead is saved to the CRM, and a personalized email is sent automatically.

The Result

Manual data entry dropped by about 70%, and every lead now gets a response within minutes, at any hour.
