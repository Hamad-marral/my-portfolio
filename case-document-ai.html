The Challenge

Important details such as invoices, contracts and forms were read and typed into spreadsheets by hand, which was slow and error-prone.

The Solution

A LangChain agent with an OpenAI model reads each uploaded file, extracts structured fields, saves them to Supabase and builds summary reports.

The Result

Processing is about 90% faster, extraction is consistently accurate, and the data connects to other tools through three integrations.
